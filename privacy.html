Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aegisfall: Crystal Guard (“the App”) handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account and do not directly collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or other personal information. Game progress, selected hero, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device.

Rewarded Advertisements

The App may offer optional rewarded advertisements, such as an opportunity to continue a game after a defeat. These ads are provided by Yandex Mobile Ads. The advertising provider may process device and usage information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising, in accordance with its own privacy policy. Please review the Yandex Privacy Policy for details.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional. You may continue to use the core game without viewing an ad.

Tracking

The App does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access or store the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). Any data handling by an advertising provider is governed by that provider’s policies and the choices available on your device.

Data Storage and Security

Your local game settings and progress remain on your device unless you remove the App or clear its data. We do not operate an account system or a server for storing player profiles.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can address the concern.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the App Store support channel.
